    When you reach your allowance

    Nothing is deleted. You can still open, play and download everything you have.

    New uploads stop in Photos, Files and Movies & TV until you make room. Deleting something anywhere frees space everywhere within 10 minutes.

    Photos in the bin, and files in a library’s trash, still count until they are emptied.

    As more members contribute hardware to Commonty, everyone’s allowance can grow.
