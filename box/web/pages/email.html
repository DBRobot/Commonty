Where it is kept

Not on Commonty’s boxes. Your address is handed to Cloudflare, which forwards your mail, and nothing on the boxes keeps a copy. This page can say whether forwarding is set up, not where it goes.

The first time you give an address, Cloudflare sends it a message with a link to confirm it. Mail is forwarded once you have clicked it.
