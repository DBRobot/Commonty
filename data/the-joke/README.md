# The joke

Do not read this in full.

It comes in parts, as it was translated: one part to each translator, so
that no one ever sees the whole of it.

## Where it comes from

The text in the `part-*.json` files is taken from the
[Monty Python Wiki](https://montypython.fandom.com/wiki/Main_Page) at
Fandom, by its contributors (the history of each article is on its page
there). It was taken on 2026-10-12 by [`scripts/the-joke`](../../scripts/the-joke),
which keeps each article's title and section headings and turns its
wikitext into plain text: templates, tables, images, references and links
are taken out, and long sections are cut short. Nothing else is changed.
