# Joining Commonty

## Who can join

For now, Commonty is open to:

- people who host a box: a computer that adds real storage or computing
  power to the network ([Hosting a box](hosting.md));
- people David invites himself.

Either way, you join with an invite code from David. Friends you invite
to play games join as guests, without a code ([Inviting friends to
games](guests.md)). The full rules are in the [Terms of use](../policies/terms.md).

## 1. Get the app

Download the Commonty app from [commonty.org/download](https://commonty.org/download). It is there for
Linux, Windows and Android. There is no app for Mac or iPhone yet.

- On Linux, the AppImage needs no install: make it executable and run it.
- On Android, your phone asks once to allow installing from outside its
  store. There is no store listing.

Each download is listed, with its fingerprint (SHA-256), in a list signed
with the same key that signs what the boxes run.

## 2. Make your account with your invite code

1. Open the app and choose **Create an account with their code**.
2. Pick a name: lowercase letters, digits, dashes, dots or underscores.
   Your name is how others see you, and it cannot be changed.
3. Enter your month and year of birth. You must be at least 13. If you
   are 13 to 17, a parent or guardian has to agree. Your answer is only
   checked, never kept. If you are under 13, the app says so and will
   not make an account on that device.
4. Tick **I agree to the Terms of use and the Privacy policy**. It starts
   unticked. Your account records which version of the terms you agreed
   to.
5. Enter your invite code and press **Create my account**.

Your account's keys are made on this device and stay there. The invite
code goes only into the app, never into a web page.

## 3. Write down your recovery key

The app then shows your recovery key once. Write it on paper and keep it
somewhere safe. It is the only way back in if every device is lost, and
nobody can make you another. Press **I wrote it down** when you have.
See [Your recovery key](recovery-key.md).

## 4. Set up a passkey

On the app's **This device** screen, press **Set up a passkey**. Your
browser opens and asks to make one. The passkey is how you sign in on the
website. It holds none of your keys. See [Signing in](signing-in.md).

## 5. You are in

An invite code from David lets you in straight away. The boxes have to
agree on your new account first, which normally takes seconds. If they
are slow, the app says it was sent but not agreed yet; it goes through
once they agree. While the network that agrees on accounts is down,
nobody can sign up.

Press **Open your services** in the app to start.

## Signing up in a browser instead

The website has its own **Create your account** page. It asks the same
month and year of birth, and the same **I agree** box, then makes an
account with a passkey and nothing else:

- It cannot take an invite code. Codes go only into the app.
- It holds no keys, so it cannot open Files, Movies & TV, Photos,
  Passwords or anything else that is locked.
- After signing up you see **Your account is made**: nothing is open to
  you yet.

Such an account is kept on one box only, and becomes a real account in
one of two ways:

- a member sends you a friend link and you open it: you become their
  guest, for games only ([Inviting friends to games](guests.md));
- David adds you as a member.

If neither happens within 7 days, it is deleted and the name is free
again. A browser-made account has no recovery key: if you are later made
a member this way, the device you use is your only way back in. Moving
such an account to keys on a device is not built yet.
