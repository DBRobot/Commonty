# Contacts

## What it is

Everyone you keep a number or an address for, whether or not they are
on Commonty, kept on the boxes. Every contact is locked on your device
before it leaves it, so no box can read a name, a number or an address.

## Using it

- **In the app:** open **Contacts** from the home page.
- **In a browser:** open Contacts. The page says it opens through the
  Commonty app; press **Open** and allow the browser in the app
  ([Using a browser](../browser.md)).
- **On a phone:** use the Android app. There is no app for iPhone yet.

Then:

- **Find someone:** type part of a name, a number or an email.
- **New contact:** **New contact**. A contact can have several phone
  numbers and emails, an address, a birthday and notes.
- **Commonty name:** if they are a member, put their name here and the
  contact says **On Commonty**. It is only a note for you; it gives them
  nothing.
- **Change or delete:** pick someone, then **Edit** or **Delete**.
- **Address books:** **New address book** makes another; pick one on the
  left to see only those.
- **Bring contacts in, or take them out:** **Import .vcf** takes a
  contacts file (Google Contacts and most phones export one), and
  **Export .vcf** saves the ones shown.

Birthdays show in [Calendar](calendar.md) when you tick **Birthdays**
there.

A deleted contact stays in your library's trash for 30 days.

## In other apps

Your contacts work in the contacts app already on your computer
(Thunderbird, GNOME, Apple's), through the Commonty app. In the
Commonty app's window, open Contacts and press **Other apps**: add a CardDAV
account in your contacts app with the address, name and password shown.
It works while Commonty runs on that computer. On an Android phone,
DAVx5 takes the same.
