# Calendar

## What it is

Your calendars, and the ones other members share with you, kept on the
boxes. Every event is locked on your device before it leaves it, so no
box can read a title, a time or a place.

## Using it

- **In the app:** open **Calendar** from the home page.
- **In a browser:** open Calendar. The page says it opens through the
  Commonty app; press **Open** and allow the browser in the app
  ([Using a browser](../browser.md)).
- **On a phone:** use the Android app. There is no app for iPhone yet.

Then:

- **See your week or month:** **Week** and **Month**, **Today**, and the
  arrows. On a phone the page is a list of days. Click an event to see
  it.
- **New event:** **New event**. Pick the calendar, whether it repeats,
  and a reminder.
- **Change or delete an event:** click it, then **Edit** or **Delete**.
  For an event that repeats, choose this one, this and later ones, or
  every one.
- **Calendars:** tick or untick one on the left to show or hide it.
  **New calendar** makes another.
- **Share a calendar:** **New shared calendar** makes one with the
  members you name; tick **Can change it** for someone who may add and
  change events. **Sharing** beside it changes who. Sharing is done in
  the Commonty app's own window, on the device that holds your account's
  main key.
- **Birthdays:** tick **Birthdays** to see the birthdays in your
  [contacts](contacts.md).
- **Bring a calendar in:** **Import .ics** takes a calendar file, such as
  Google Calendar's export.

If an event changed on another of your devices while you were editing
it, the page says so and shows it as it now is, and your change is not
saved; make it again.

A deleted event stays in your library's trash for 30 days.
