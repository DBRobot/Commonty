# Settings

Settings is under your name in the bar, or **Settings** in the app. It has
seven tabs. The parts that hold your keys (your device names and disk
images) open through the Commonty app ([Using a browser](browser.md)).

## Profile

- **Account:** your name, which cannot be changed; your email address
  here and where it forwards to ([Your email address](email.md)); and
  whether you are a member or a guest.
- **Signing in:** how many devices and passkeys you have, and whether
  you have a recovery key ([Your recovery key](recovery-key.md)).

## Devices

- **Signed in now:** every browser and app signed in as you, with what
  kind it is and when it was last used. Sign one out, or press **Sign out
  everywhere else**.
- **Devices with a key:** the devices that hold your keys, and the
  passkeys you sign in with. **Show names** opens the names, which are
  locked. **Add a device** shows a code to scan from the new one;
  **Remove** takes a device or passkey off your account and signs it out
  everywhere ([Your devices](devices.md), [Signing in](signing-in.md)).

## Storage

How much of your allowance each service uses: Photos, Files and Movies &
TV (with your disk images), Code and Passwords. When you reach it, nothing is deleted;
new uploads stop until you make room, and freeing space anywhere frees it
everywhere within 10 minutes. Things in a trash still count until it is
emptied.

## Network

- **Your home's boxes:** the boxes in your house and whether they are up.
  **Moved or stolen?** is for a box that has left your house: you approve
  it starting up somewhere new, or mark it stolen so it never unlocks
  again.
- **Wi-Fi:** the network your boxes use. Change your Wi-Fi password here
  before you change it on the router, so a box on Wi-Fi is not cut off
  ([Your home network and Wi-Fi](network.md)).
- **Ad blocking at home:** whether it is on, and how to point your router
  at it ([Ad blocking](services/ad-blocking.md)).

## Backups

Your disk images: copies of your computers. Download one, or delete it to
the trash ([Backups of your computers](backups.md)).

## This device

**Show the assistant:** Ask Commonty, the round button in the lower
right corner of each page, on this device. Turn it off and it is gone
from every page here; turn it back on any time ([The site,
drawn](site-map.md)).

## Leave

Take everything with you, and leave Commonty ([Leaving](leaving.md)).

- **Take your things with you:** Files and Movies & TV as one zip, each
  disk image, all your photos as one zip, and your passwords from
  Passwords' own Export screen.
- **Leave Commonty:** every sign-in ends and everything of yours is
  deleted at once. This cannot be undone.
