# Your recovery key

## The paper key

When you make your account, the app shows your recovery key once. Write
it on paper and keep it somewhere safe, away from your devices.

- It is not stored anywhere: not on a box, not on your device, not with
  us.
- Nobody can issue another one for the same account.
- Settings shows whether your account has one, never the key itself.

An account made in a browser has no recovery key ([Joining
Commonty](joining.md#signing-up-in-a-browser-instead)).

## What it opens

Your recovery key is a key like your devices' keys. It opens:

- your libraries: Files, Movies & TV;
- the password for your disk images;
- your Photos password;
- your account: it can replace your account's main key.

It does not open Passwords. Your vault opens with its own master
password, which only you know.

## Using it

If every device is lost or stolen:

1. Install the app on a new device, from [commonty.org/download](https://commonty.org/download).
2. Choose **Recover with your recovery key**.
3. Enter your name and your recovery key, and press **Recover**.

What happens:

- this device gets a new main key and becomes your only device;
- every old device and every passkey is removed, so they are signed out;
- your libraries are opened with the paper key and locked again to the
  new keys;
- you get a **new** recovery key. Write it down: the old one stops
  working.

Then set up a passkey again from the app ([Signing in](signing-in.md)).

After recovering, your account has a new main key, and you are still a
member: the new key is vouched for by your recovery key, so every box
lets it in as you, whether you joined with an invite code or David added
you. Nobody without your recovery key can do the same. If David removed
you, recovering does not undo that.

## Nobody can recover your data without it

Your data is locked on your own devices with keys only you hold. We do
not have them, and neither does David, nor whoever looks after a box.

If you lose every device and your recovery key, what is locked stays
locked: Files, Movies & TV, Photos and your disk images. A passkey does
not help, because a passkey only signs you in and holds no keys. Nobody
can get your data back. That is the same lock that keeps everyone else
out. See the [Privacy policy](../policies/privacy.md).

If you lose your Passwords master password, your vault cannot be opened
either, with or without the recovery key.

## Keeping it safe

- Anyone with your recovery key and your name can take over your account
  and read your locked data. Keep it as you would a house key.
- Do not keep it in Commonty: you would need it to get in.
- If you think someone has seen it, recover your account yourself to get
  a new one. Be aware this removes your other devices and passkeys.
