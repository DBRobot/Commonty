# Inviting friends to games

You can invite friends who are not members to play on your game servers.
They join as your **guests**.

## Sending a game link

1. Open **Friends** and press **Make a friend link**.
2. Copy the link and send it however you like. It works once, for a day.
   You can have up to 20 waiting at a time.
3. Your friend opens it. If they have no account, it signs them up first:
   they pick a name, give their month and year of birth (checked, never
   kept; at least 13, and from 13 to 17 a parent or guardian agrees),
   tick **I agree to the Terms of use and the Privacy policy**, and make
   a passkey in their browser. No invite code, no app needed for that
   step.
4. On your game server's page, tick your friend under who plays.

A link opened by someone who is already a member makes you friends, and
they stay a member.

## What your guest does

To play, a guest needs the Commonty app, because game servers are only
reachable on Commonty's own network:

1. Install the app, from [commonty.org/download](https://commonty.org/download), on the computer or phone they play on (Linux, Windows
   or Android; there is no Mac or iPhone app yet).
2. Add it as a device: in the browser, Settings, Devices, **Add a
   device**; in the app, **Have a code?**, then approve with the passkey
   ([Your devices](devices.md)).
3. Turn on the network in the app, open Games, and find your server
   under **Invited to**. Its page shows the address to type into the
   game.

## What a guest can and cannot do

A guest can:

- see and join the game servers you invited them to, while they run;
- see their own Settings: profile, devices and sign-ins.

A guest cannot:

- use Files, Movies & TV, Photos, Passwords, Chat, Git, Metrics or any
  other service;
- start, stop or change a server, see its settings or its log, or create
  servers of their own;
- invite anyone to a server.

A box opens a server's ports only to the devices of its owner and the
people invited to it.

## Guests are free, and the terms cover them

Guests pay nothing, like members. The [Terms of use](../policies/terms.md)
apply to guests too: nothing illegal, no harassing anyone, no attacking
the network, and no selling or giving away access. If something goes
wrong, it is handled as it is for anyone.

## When it ends

- Untick a guest on a server's page, and the box closes it to their
  devices within about 20 seconds.
- A guest stays a guest only while a member is still their friend. When
  no member is, they are let in nowhere.
- A browser sign-up that never becomes a guest is deleted after 7 days.
- A guest can leave whenever they like, in Settings, **Leave** tab
  ([Leaving](leaving.md#guests-and-accounts-still-waiting)).

## Privacy

A game server has to read its world to run it. Worlds, saves and logs are
👁 **Open to the box** and 🏠 **Stays home**. A game's log can hold what
players type, so only the server's owner sees it. Guests' sign-ins are
like members': 👁 **Open to the box** and 📖 **Read outside**, with
nothing secret in them. See the
[Privacy policy](../policies/privacy.md).
