# Leaving

You can leave whenever you like. When you do, everything of yours is
deleted from every box straight away, and it cannot be undone. Copies in
the boxes' backups expire on their own: within 90 days nothing is left
anywhere.

## Taking your things with you

Do this first: once you leave, your services no longer open and
everything in them is deleted.

In Settings, open the **Leave** tab. Under **Take your things with you**,
each one downloads straight from there:

- **Files & Movies:** **Download everything** saves your whole library
  as one zip, with its folders and names. It is unlocked on your device
  as it downloads.
- **Disk images:** each one is listed with its own **Download**, as it
  is.
- **Photos:** **Download all photos** saves every photo and video as one
  zip, through the app, in a folder for each album. A photo in no album
  is in **Not in an album**; a photo in several albums is in the first of
  them by name.
- **Passwords:** **Open Export** opens your vault's own **Export** screen.
  Type your master password to export. The file holds every password in
  the clear, so keep it safe and delete it when you are done.

Each download shows how far it has got and about how long is left, and
**Cancel** stops it. In the app's own window the zips are saved in your
Downloads folder; in a browser they arrive as ordinary downloads. In a
browser, press **Open them** first: they use the keys on your devices,
so the app on this computer opens the tab in the same browser
([Using a browser](browser.md)).

You can also take things out at any time without leaving: download from
Files, Photos or Backups as usual.

## Pressing Leave

1. In Settings, **Leave** tab, take your things with you first: the
   downloads are right above the button.
2. Press **Leave and delete everything**. The box that opens says
   plainly that everything is deleted now and cannot be undone.
3. Confirm with your passkey, or in the app if your account's main key is
   on a device. An account whose main key is a passkey leaves from the
   website, signed in with it; one whose key is on a device leaves from
   the app's own window (not a browser you let in from it, since this
   cannot be undone).

## Every sign-in ends at once

From that moment, on every box:

- every page, app and service refuses you;
- every sign-in of yours ends, including Passwords;
- your devices leave Commonty's network;
- friend links you sent stop working;
- your game servers stop.

## Everything is deleted at once

There is no waiting time and no coming back. Within minutes each box
deletes what it holds of yours: your files, films and disk images (trash
included), photos, passwords vault, your own repositories and Git
account, game servers and their worlds, your email address here, where
you stopped watching, friendships and sign-in history. If you sign in
again, Settings shows only the **Leave** tab, with the date you left.

## Gone from backups within 90 days

Copies in the boxes' backups and snapshots cannot be picked out and
deleted; they run out on their own. Within 90 days of leaving, nothing
of yours is left on any box or in any backup.

## What stays

- **A record of your name.** It holds your name and your public keys, so
  that nobody else can take the name. It is part of the record every box
  agrees on, which is never shortened. Members can read it, and
  Cloudflare can see it when a device fetches it. It holds no
  data of yours: any keys in it are locked to keys only you held, for
  libraries that no longer exist. Commonty's network also keeps an empty
  entry under your name, with no devices.
- **What you gave to other people's repositories.** Code, issues and
  comments in someone else's repository stay there, as on any
  open-source project, shown under a placeholder account.

## Guests, and accounts still waiting

Anyone can leave, not only members. A friend's guest, and an account made
on the website that is still waiting to be let in, leave the same way:
Settings, **Leave** tab, **Leave and delete everything**, confirmed with
the passkey.
(An account still waiting sees only that tab, and its waiting page links
to it.)

- Every sign-in ends at once, the guest's devices leave the network, and
  the game servers they were invited to close to them.
- Their account is deleted straight away, with their friendships, their
  guest access and any game servers of theirs. There is no coming back.
  Nothing of anyone else's is touched: a member they were friends with
  keeps everything, other guests included.
- An account still waiting is kept on one box only, and is deleted
  straight away. Its name is then free again.

## Being suspended

Nobody can remove you; only you can leave. David can suspend a member who
breaks the [Terms of use](../policies/terms.md). Action only starts from a
report, because Commonty cannot see what you keep locked. Before anything
is done, you are told what was reported and given a chance to show you are
using Commonty properly.

If you are suspended:

- every sign-in ends at once, as above;
- the reason is written in the signed member list, where you and everyone
  can read it, and your home page shows it and the date it runs to;
- for 90 days you can still sign in to open and take your own files, and
  nothing else: nothing of yours is read, changed or deleted in that time.
  What Passwords keeps is on your own devices, as always;
- only David can lift it, and within the 90 days nothing is lost;
- after the 90 days your things are deleted the same way as on leaving,
  and gone from every box and backup within 90 days of that.
