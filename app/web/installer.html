Choose a card

The card goes into the Raspberry Pi. Put it in this computer's card reader, or a USB reader, and pick it here.

Start from a backup

Your home controllers' backups are locked so that only your devices can open them. Pick one to put its devices, automations and people back on the new card.

    Write the card

    The card is checked against this release's signature before anything is written.

    Getting the release Working

    ——

    Start the Pi

    1. Take the card out of this computer and put it (back) in the Raspberry Pi.
    2. Connect the Pi to your router with a network cable. It has to be wired: it unlocks its card from your home's box before it starts.
    3. Plug in the Pi's power.
    Looking for it on your home network Looking

    The first start takes a few minutes: it sets up its locked card and starts again. If it is not found after ten minutes, check that this computer is on the same home network as the router the Pi is plugged into, and that the Pi's lights show it has power.

    Pair it with your account

    Your home controller learns who you are, gets its own name and joins your Commonty network. Only people you let in can open it; no box can.
