Choose a USB drive

The new box starts from this drive and sets itself up. Put a USB drive of 8 GB or more in this computer, and pick it here.

Wi-Fi for the box

A box plugged into your router by cable needs none. Otherwise it joins this network, and keeps the password locked to its own chip.

Write the drive

The installer is checked against this release's signature before anything is written. Then the drive gets a one-use pass for your account, good for a week.

Getting the release Working

——

Start the new box from the drive

  1. Take the drive out of this computer and put it in the new box.
  2. Clear Secure Boot's keys in the box's firmware settings first, or it won't start from this drive: under Boot or Security, “Reset to setup mode”, “Clear Secure Boot keys” or “Delete all keys”. Leave Secure Boot itself on.
  3. Turn the box on and open its boot menu: usually F12, F11, F10 or Esc while it starts. Pick the USB drive.
  4. Follow its screen. It checks the machine's settings, offers to back up its old disk to your images, and installs. Nothing more is asked here.
Waiting for the box to join Waiting

You can close this window. The pass on the drive works for a week; once the box has joined, its old disk's backup appears in Settings, Backups, on any of your devices.
